# 5. Instagram Posts, Carousels & Photo Ideas

Carousels currently get some of the highest engagement on Instagram, because people swipe (more time on the post) and save. Instagram also shows a carousel again with slide 2 if someone skipped it the first time, so you get two chances.

**Carousel rules**
- Slide 1 = the hook (big text over a strong photo). It decides everything.
- Use 6 to 10 slides with one idea per slide and short text.
- The last slide is the CTA ("Save this", "Follow for more", "Comment X").
- Format: 4:5 portrait (1080x1350). It takes up the most space in the feed.
- Keep a consistent design (the same 2 fonts and the same colors), so people recognize your posts instantly.

## Carousel 1 – "A Week in the Life of a Pro Cyclist" (in numbers)
- **Slide 1:** You on the bike, epic landscape. TEXT: "One week as a pro cyclist. In numbers."
- **Slide 2:** "Kilometers ridden: 812 km"
- **Slide 3:** "Hours on the bike: 26h 40min"
- **Slide 4:** "Climbing: 14,300 m (that's 1.6 x Everest)"
- **Slide 5:** "Calories burned: 31,500 kcal"
- **Slide 6:** "Espressos: 19 ☕"
- **Slide 7:** "Massages: 4. Naps: 6. Laundry loads: 5"
- **Slide 8:** "Hardest moment: 3 x 15 min at threshold in the rain"
- **Slide 9:** "Best moment: [sunset descent photo]"
- **Slide 10:** "How many km did you ride this week? 👇 Save this to compare next month."

**Caption:**
> 812 km, 19 espressos and 1 rain session I'll never forget. This is a normal training week for me in the build-up to [race]. What did your week look like? 👇
> #procycling #cycling #trainingweek #roadcycling #cyclinglife

## Carousel 3 – "How to Fuel a 4-Hour Ride Like a Pro" (high saves)
- **Slide 1:** Flat lay of food + bottles. TEXT: "How to fuel a 4-hour ride (like a pro)"
- **Slide 2:** "Night before: a carb-focused dinner (pasta/rice), don't stuff yourself"
- **Slide 3:** "Breakfast, 2 to 3 hours before: porridge, toast, honey, coffee"
- **Slide 4:** "First hour: start eating after 30 min. Real food (rice cake, bar)"
- **Slide 5:** "Every hour: 60 to 90 g carbs (bottles + gels/bars)"
- **Slide 6:** "Last hour: gels + caffeine before the hardest part"
- **Slide 7:** "After: carbs + protein within 30 to 60 min"
- **Slide 8:** "Example plan for 4 hours" (a table: hour 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 with what you eat)
- **Slide 9:** "Save this before your next long ride 📌"

*(Add a short line: "Train your gut slowly. If you normally eat 30 g/h, don't jump straight to 90.")*

## Carousel 4 – "Pro vs Amateur: Training Week Comparison"
Two-column slides (amateur | pro): hours per week, rest days, % easy riding, strength training, sleep, nutrition, recovery tools. Last slide: "You don't need pro hours. You need pro habits."

---

## Carousel 5 – "My Race Bike, Explained" (Gear / brand-friendly)
One slide per component with a close-up detail photo and 1 to 2 lines of text, including why you choose that setup (tire pressure, crank length, bar width). End with "Ask me anything about my setup 👇". Tag the brands (team sponsors). They often reshare, which brings reach plus a relationship.

---

## Carousel 6 – "Before & After: 10 Years of Cycling"
Slide 1: you as a kid on a bike vs you today. Then the years: first race, first win, first crash, juniors, U23, first pro contract, Grand Tour, now. Emotional, shareable and great for people who are new to your account.

---

## Carousel 7 – "Ranking the Best Climbs I've Ever Ridden"
One climb per slide with your photo + stats (length, gradient, your time, a fun fact, a rating out of 10). People save these for bucket lists, and tourism boards and travel brands notice them (that's a potential partnership).

## Carousel 8 – "What Pro Cyclists Actually Do on Rest Days"
A meme-style carousel. Funny, relatable, and good for sharing.

## Carousel 9 – "Workout of the Week" (a recurring series)
- Slide 1: "Steal my workout 🔥 [Name of workout]"
- Slide 2: The structure as a graphic
- Slide 3: "If your FTP is 200 / 250 / 300, here are your watts"
- Slide 4: Pro tips for doing it right
- Slide 5: "Do it and tag me @[handle] 📲"
Post it every week. Series build habits ("I'm waiting for Monday's workout").

## Carousel 10 – "Answering Your Questions" (from a Story Q&A)
Collect questions from a Story Q&A box. Screenshot the question, then add your answer as text on a photo. Easy content that builds community, because people love seeing their question featured.

## Photo ideas: your shot list 📸
Collect these during the season. Aim for a mix of **action, portrait, detail and lifestyle**.

### Action shots
1. Out of the saddle on a steep climb, shot from low and in front
2. A descent with a lean angle, shot from the outside of the corner
3. Panning shot at speed (slow shutter, sharp rider, blurred background)
4. Sprint/attack, face in pain
5. Riding in the rain, water spraying off your wheels
6. Silhouette against a sunset/sunrise on a ridge
7. A drone shot of a winding road with you as a small dot (the "scale" shot)
8. Riding through a crowd of fans at a race

### Portraits
9. A post-race portrait with a dirty/salty face (the "this is the job" shot)
10. Sitting on the curb, exhausted, bike next to you
11. The podium / celebration moment
12. Laughing with teammates
13. A clean "studio-like" portrait in team kit (for your media kit and profile)
14. A casual portrait without the bike (shows the human side)

### Detail shots
15. Hands on the handlebar (tape, gloves, race number on the wrist)
16. Legs: tan lines, veins, road rash (people love and hate it)
17. Shoes clipping into pedals
18. A race number being pinned
19. Bike computer screen with stats
20. Drops of sweat on the top tube
21. Espresso on a café table with your helmet next to it

### Lifestyle
22. A café stop with the bike leaning against the wall (the classic)
23. Training camp hotel balcony view
24. A big plate of pasta + your hand
25. A packed suitcase + bike box at the airport
26. Ice bath / massage table
27. You washing your bike
28. Your home setup: indoor trainer with a view
29. With family / partner / dog
30. Your morning routine: coffee, journaling, the training plan on a phone

### Photo dumps (very popular for authenticity)
Post one 10-photo dump each month, for example "September dump" or "Camp dump". Mix 2 to 3 strong photos with funny, random, unpolished ones (food, a hotel room mess, a teammate sleeping, a sunset, a selfie with a fan). Lead with the strongest photo. Caption idea: "September: 2,900 km, 1 crash, 0 regrets, way too much pasta."

## Caption templates

**1. Storytelling caption (for emotional or race posts)**
```
[One-line hook that creates curiosity]
[2 to 4 short lines: what happened, how it felt]
[The lesson or reflection]
[Question for the audience]
[3 to 5 hashtags]
```
**Example:**
> I've never been this empty on a bike.
> km 170, the final climb, and my legs just said no. I watched the group ride away and I couldn't do anything about it.
> But that's the deal in this sport. You don't get to choose your good days, only how you respond to the bad ones.
> What's your worst day on the bike? 👇
> #procycling #cycling #roadcycling

**2. Value caption (for tips/carousels)**
```
[Promise: "Save this before your next ride"]
[3 to 5 bullet points of the key info]
[CTA: "Send this to the friend who always bonks"]
```

**3. Short caption (for aesthetic photos)**
> "Office for the day 🏔️ 167 km | 3,400 m"
> "Some days the legs are good. Today the coffee was better ☕"
> "Lost in [place], found in my Zone 2."

## Stories: the daily habit (5 to 10 frames per day)
Stories don't go viral, but they build the relationship that brands pay for (brands look at your Story views!).
- **Morning:** breakfast + "Today's training: ___" (with a poll: "Would you survive this? Yes/No")
- **During the ride:** 2 to 3 short clips (views, effort, funny moments)
- **After:** a screenshot of the ride stats + an emoji slider "How hard was this?"
- **Evening:** dinner, recovery, a question box ("Ask me anything about pro cycling")
- **Weekly:** a "This or That" quiz, "Guess my watts", a behind-the-scenes look at brand partnerships
- **Story Highlights:** Race Days · What I Eat · Training · Gear · Q&A · Partners (with codes)
