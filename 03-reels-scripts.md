# 3. Reels / Shorts Scripts (15 complete scripts)

**How to read these scripts**
- `[SHOT]` = what the camera films
- `TEXT:` = on-screen text
- `VO:` = voice-over (record it after filming, quietly and close to the mic)
- `SAY:` = you talking to camera
- Cut every 1 to 3 seconds. Add captions/subtitles to EVERY Reel (most people watch on mute).
- Export at 9:16, 1080x1920. Use trending audio quietly underneath your voice (10 to 20% volume), or the trending audio alone for no-talk Reels.

## Reel 2 – "Pro vs Amateur: Same Climb" (Pillar: Training & Data) ⭐ Viral classic
**Length:** 20 to 30 sec | **Setup:** Ride a local climb with an amateur friend. Film both bike computers.

| Time | Shot | Text / Voice |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2s | Split screen: both of you at the bottom of the climb | TEXT: "Pro vs amateur. Same climb. Who's faster? 👀" |
| 2–5s | Start together | VO: "This is Tom. He's ridden for 5 years, FTP 260. I'm a pro. 5 km climb, 7%." |
| 5–10s | Cuts between both computers (watts, HR) | TEXT: "Tom: 270 W / 172 bpm" vs "Me: 270 W / 128 bpm" VO: "Same power at the start. Look at the heart rates." |
| 10–15s | You ride away, Tom fights | TEXT: "Minute 4: I go to my threshold" |
| 15–20s | Times on screen | TEXT: "Tom: 21:14 / Me: 14:02" |
| 20–25s | Both of you at the top laughing. Tom: "Never again." | SAY: "The difference isn't magic. It's 30,000 km a year." |
| 25–28s | — | TEXT: "Comment your time on your local climb ⬇️" |

**Caption:**
> Same climb, 7 minutes difference 😅 Respect to Tom for doing this. The gap between amateur and pro is mostly volume, consistency and recovery, not just talent. Want me to do this with a female rider, a junior or a triathlete next?
> #procycling #cycling #provsamateur #climbing #roadbike

## Reel 12 – "How Much Do Pro Cyclists Earn?" (Pillar: Real Talk) ⭐ Controversial = viral
**Length:** 30 to 40 sec | **Format:** Talking head, sitting at home or on the team bus
**Note:** You don't have to reveal your own salary. Talk about ranges and public information (minimum salaries are set by the UCI and the riders' associations. Check the current numbers before posting).

```
[0–2s]  SAY: "How much does a pro cyclist actually earn? Let's talk money."
        TEXT: "Pro cyclist salary 💰 (honest)"
[2–10s] SAY: "Most people think we're all millionaires. The top stars? Yes, millions. But most of the peloton…"
[10–20s] TEXT on screen showing the tiers: "WorldTour minimum: €[X]" / "ProTeam minimum: €[X]" / "Continental: sometimes €0 to [X]"
        VO: "There are minimum salaries in the WorldTour, a bit less in ProTeams, and at continental level many riders earn very little or nothing."
[20–30s] SAY: "And our careers are short. Average maybe 5 to 8 years. That's why I'm building this account. For after."
[30–35s] SAY: "So if you follow me, you're literally part of my retirement plan." *laugh*
        TEXT: "Follow to support 🙏"
```
**Why it works:** curiosity + controversy + a personal "why" = comments, shares and follows.
