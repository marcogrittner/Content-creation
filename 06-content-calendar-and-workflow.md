# 6. Content Calendar & Workflow

## 6.1 Standard weekly plan (training weeks)

| Day | Instagram | YouTube | Stories |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Monday** | Carousel: "Workout of the week" | — | Training plan, poll |
| **Tuesday** | Reel: Training / Data (e.g. "What 400 W feels like") | Short (repost) | Ride clips |
| **Wednesday** | Reel: Food (e.g. "What I eat") | **Long video goes live (e.g. 5 p.m. local time)** | Promote the video |
| **Thursday** | Reel: Humor / POV | Short | Q&A box |
| **Friday** | Carousel or photo post (tips / real talk) | Short | Weekend plans |
| **Saturday** | Reel: Pro life / aesthetic (e.g. camp, race prep) | Short | Big ride / race |
| **Sunday** | Photo dump or a storytelling post (optional) | Community post (poll) | Recap of the week |

**Best posting times (a starting point; check your own Insights after 4 weeks):** Instagram from 7 to 9 a.m. or 6 to 9 p.m. in your audience's main time zone. YouTube from 3 to 6 p.m. Consistency matters more than the exact time.

## 6.2 Race week / Grand Tour plan (low time, high access)

When racing, you have little time but the most unique content. Do this:
- **Daily:** Stories only (2 to 3 min of effort). Ask a teammate or soigneur to film one 10-second clip of you.
- **Every 2 to 3 days:** a "race day in 30 seconds" Reel (film only, an editor puts it together).
- **During a Grand Tour:** "Rest day vlog" on YouTube (short, 8 to 10 min).
- **After the race:** the long race vlog + a carousel "What 3 weeks of racing did to my body".
- **Pre-produce:** before a big race block, film 4 to 6 evergreen Reels (tips, humor) and schedule them in Meta Business Suite / YouTube Studio.

## 6.3 Your first 30 days (launch plan)

**Week 1 – Set up the foundation**
- Optimize your bio (see file 01), switch to a Professional/Creator account, and create Story Highlights.
- YouTube: channel banner, trailer (a 60-sec "who I am and what you'll see here" video), and a profile picture that matches Instagram.
- Post: 1 introduction carousel "Hi, I'm [Name]. Pro cyclist for [Team]. Here's what you'll see on this account" plus 3 Reels (food, pro vs amateur, POV humor).

**Week 2 – Test formats**
- 4 Reels from different pillars + 2 carousels. Publish YouTube video #1: "What a pro cyclist eats in a day".
- Reply to EVERY comment within the first hour of posting (the algorithm boosts posts with active conversations).

**Week 3 – Double down**
- Check Insights: which Reel had the most shares and the best watch time? Make 2 more versions of that format.
- YouTube video #2: "Pro vs amateur".
- Do one collab Reel with a teammate or another creator (the Instagram "Collab" feature puts the post on both profiles, so it reaches both audiences).

**Week 4 – Community & series**
- Launch a recurring series (e.g. "Workout Wednesday", "Rate my bike setup", "Undercover Pro").
- YouTube video #3: Real talk / story.
- A Story Q&A, which turns into a carousel + a YouTube Q&A later.
- Create a simple media kit (see file 07), even with a small following.

## 6.4 The filming workflow (made for a busy athlete)

**Daily (5 to 10 minutes)**
- Phone in your jersey pocket, and/or an Insta360/GoPro on the bars. Film 3 to 5 short clips: the view, the effort, the food, a funny moment.
- Always film **both vertical (for Reels) and horizontal (for YouTube)**, or film in 4K horizontal and crop. An Insta360 makes this easy.
- Say one or two sentences to camera every day. You'll use them as hooks later.

**Weekly batch (on the rest day, 2 to 3 hours)**
- Edit 4 to 5 Reels in CapCut or Instagram Edits. Use templates so it's fast.
- Write captions and schedule everything.
- Film 1 talking-head segment (tips/real talk) for the next week.

**Organization**
- One folder per month in Google Drive / iCloud, with subfolders: `Food`, `Training`, `Race`, `Funny`, `Scenery`, `Talking`.
- A content idea list in Notes / Notion. When an idea comes up on the bike, add it right away.

**When to hire help (the most important investment)**
- Once you earn your first brand deals (or from the start, if you can afford it), hire a **freelance video editor** (search Upwork, Fiverr, or ask cycling-creator communities). Budget: roughly €30 to 100 per Reel and €150 to 500 per YouTube video depending on quality. That lets you send raw footage and keep focusing on racing.
- Later: a manager/agent for brand deals (they usually take 15 to 25%).

## 6.5 Season planning (cycling has seasons, so your content should too)

| Period | Content focus |
|---|---|
| **Nov–Dec (off-season)** | Off-season life, holidays, gym, "what pros do when not racing", Q&A, story-time videos, gear unboxings (new season bikes/kit). This is a great time for long YouTube videos because you have time. |
| **Jan–Feb (training camps)** | Camp vlogs, aesthetic Reels, team presentation, new kit reveal, "how I train in winter". |
| **Mar–Apr (Classics / early races)** | Race vlogs, recon rides, Strade-Bianche/cobbles content, gravel/weather drama. |
| **May–Jul (Grand Tours / big races)** | Race access content, daily Stories, fueling/recovery, peak hype (a lot of new people search for cycling during the Tour de France, so post the most during July!). |
| **Aug–Oct (late season)** | Reflections, end-of-season recap, transfers/contracts talk, "What next?", gravel or fun events, fan rides. |

**July hack:** During the Tour de France, the general audience is the most interested in cycling. Even if you're not racing it, post explainer content ("Tour de France explained in 60 seconds", "How riders pee during the race", "What riders eat at the Tour") and ride the search wave.

## 6.6 Track these numbers (monthly)
| Metric | Why | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Reel shares per 1,000 views | The strongest viral signal | Over 10 is good |
| Saves | Value | Grows each month |
| Follower growth | Overall health | +5 to 15% a month early on |
| YouTube CTR | Thumbnail/title quality | 6 to 10% |
| YouTube 30-second retention | The hook quality | Over 50 to 60% |
| Average view duration | Content quality | Over 40% of the video |
| Story views / followers | Community strength (brands check this) | 5 to 15% |
| Engagement rate | Brands check this | Over 3 to 6% |

Every month, ask yourself: **What were my top 3 posts? Why? How can I make 3 more versions of them?**
