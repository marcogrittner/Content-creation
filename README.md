## If you only read one page, read this

1. **Your edge is access.** Millions of people ride bikes, but almost none of them know what the inside of the pro peloton looks like. Every piece of content should answer one question: *"What is it really like to be a pro?"*
2. **Use numbers.** Watts, kcal, km, hours, euros, kg of pasta. Specific numbers stop the scroll in cycling better than anything else.
3. **Compare pro and amateur.** "Pro vs amateur" is the most reliable viral format in the niche, because every viewer measures themselves against you.
4. **Be a person, not just an athlete.** People follow you for the results, but they stay for your personality, the struggle and the honesty.
5. **Reels and Shorts bring new people in, long YouTube videos make them fans, and carousels and Stories turn them into a community.** Brands pay for that community.
6. **Minimum output:** 4 Reels, 2 carousels or photo posts, daily Stories and 1 YouTube video every week (2 per month during heavy race blocks). Reuse every Reel as a YouTube Short and a TikTok.
7. **Film everything, edit later.** Keep your phone on a chest or bar mount, film 5 to 10 minutes a day, and batch-edit on rest days (or pay an editor; see file 06).
