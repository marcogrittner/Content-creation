# 7. Monetization & Brand Deals

## 7.1 Before you sign anything: check your team contract ⚠️
Most pro teams have exclusive sponsor contracts covering categories like bikes, wheels, components, helmets, clothing, shoes, eyewear, nutrition and sometimes watches or cars. You usually **cannot** promote a competitor of a team sponsor.
- Read your contract or ask your team manager/agent which categories are **free** for personal sponsors.
- Categories that are often free: coffee, watches, headphones, recovery tools (massage guns, compression), apps (training, sleep), skincare/sunscreen, finance/insurance, travel, food (non-sports-nutrition), cars (sometimes), fashion/lifestyle wear (off-bike), betting (avoid it: it can hurt your image and may be banned).
- Team sponsors are also an opportunity: ask them for paid personal content campaigns, because they already know you.

## 7.2 Income streams (in order of when you can start)

| Stream | When to start | How it works | Potential |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Affiliate links** | From day one | Links/codes for gear you use (Amazon, cycling shops, nutrition, apps). Earn 3 to 15% per sale | Small at first, but passive |
| **Brand deals (sponsored content)** | ~5,000 to 10,000 engaged followers | Paid Reels, Stories, YouTube integrations | The biggest income source |
| **YouTube AdSense** | 1,000 subs + 4,000 watch hours (or 10 million Shorts views in 90 days) | Ads on your videos | Grows with your library |
| **Instagram/YouTube subscriptions & memberships** | ~10,000+ | Exclusive content (training files, Q&As) for a monthly fee | Steady monthly income |
| **Digital products** | ~10,000+ | Training plans, workout packs (.zwo), a fueling guide, an e-book, recipes | High margin, scales well |
| **Coaching** | Anytime | 1-on-1 or group coaching (check team rules). It works well post-career | High per client, limited by time |
| **Camps & fan rides** | ~20,000+ | "Ride with [Name]" weekends in Mallorca/Girona, with a travel company partner | Very high, and it builds community |
| **Merch** | Strong community | Cycling caps, jerseys, socks with your inside jokes / your brand | Medium |
| **Ambassador deals (long term)** | Established | 6 to 12 month contracts with a monthly fee + content | Stable income, perfect for post-career |
| **Speaking / events / commentary / media** | Post-career | TV commentary, podcast guest spots, corporate talks | Strong post-career path |

## 7.3 What brands pay (rough industry ranges, 2025/26)

These numbers vary widely by country, niche, engagement and usage rights. Use them as a starting point only.
- **Instagram Reel:** roughly €100 to 300 per 10,000 followers. Niche athletes with high engagement can charge more (a pro rider is credible, and that's worth extra).
- **Instagram Story set (3 frames + link):** around 30 to 50% of a Reel price.
- **YouTube integration (60 sec):** often €15 to 40 per 1,000 average views of your videos.
- **Dedicated YouTube video:** 2 to 3 times the integration price.
- **Add extra for:** usage rights (the brand uses your content in ads: +30 to 100%), exclusivity (you don't work with competitors), and packages (a multi-month deal is cheaper per post for the brand, but more secure for you).

## 7.4 Your media kit (one to two pages, PDF)
1. **A strong portrait** + your name + "Pro cyclist for [Team]"
2. **Short bio:** 2 to 3 lines (results, personality, what your content is about)
3. **Numbers:** followers per platform, average Reel views, average YouTube views, engagement rate, Story views
4. **Audience:** age, gender, top countries/cities (screenshots from Insights)
5. **Highlights:** 3 of your best posts (with views), your best results
6. **Previous partners** (team sponsors count!)
7. **Offers/packages:** e.g. "1 Reel + 3 Stories", "YouTube integration", "3-month ambassador"
8. **Contact:** email + manager

Make it in Canva (search "media kit" templates). Update it every 3 months.

**Tips**
- Find the right person on LinkedIn ("Influencer Marketing Manager", "Brand Manager", "Partnerships") instead of info@ emails.
- Pitch 10 brands every month, follow up once after 7 days, and expect a 10 to 20% reply rate.
- Start with brands you actually use. Authenticity sells better and protects your credibility.
- Consider creator platforms/agencies that connect athletes with brands once you have 20,000+ followers.

## 7.6 How to make sponsored content that doesn't feel like an ad
- **Integrate the product into your normal formats:** "What I eat in a day" (nutrition brand), "Recovery routine" (massage gun), "Morning routine at camp" (coffee brand), "Pack with me" (luggage/travel).
- **Hook first, product second:** never start a Reel with the product.
- **Show, don't tell:** use the product naturally and explain why YOU use it.
- **Always label it** as a paid partnership (the Instagram tool and the YouTube "includes paid promotion" box). It's required by law in most countries (EU/UK/US).

**Example – a YouTube integration script (45 sec)**
> "Quick break, because I want to tell you about something that's actually part of my day, every day. After every hard session, I use [Product] for [benefit]. As a pro I ride 30,000 km a year, so recovery isn't a luxury; it's my job. What I like about it is [specific detail]. If you want to try it, [Brand] gave my viewers [X]% off with code [CODE] at the link in the description. Okay, back to the video. Because the next part is where it hurt…"

## 7.7 Life after racing: build the asset now
Your social media is your **"career insurance"**. To make it work after racing:
1. **Build your personal brand around YOU, not only your team.** Use your own name as your handle, and your own colors and logo.
2. **Build an email list** from day one (a free training plan in exchange for an email, using a tool like Beehiiv, ConvertKit or Mailchimp). Social media platforms can change, but your email list belongs to you.
3. **Build expertise content** (training, fueling, mindset). It stays valuable when you're not racing anymore.
4. **Collect relationships:** stay in touch with every brand manager and journalist you meet.
5. **Plan the transition:** in your last season, create a "My final season" series. That kind of content gets very emotional and has huge reach, and it launches your next chapter (coach, creator, ambassador, gravel/ultra rider, commentator).
6. **Post-career content ideas:** "Ex-pro tries normal life", "My first ride as a non-pro", gravel/ultra-distance adventures, bike-packing trips, coaching amateurs to their goals (a transformation series), and hosting a podcast with other pros.

The riders who win on social media are not the ones with the best results. They're the ones who **post consistently for 12+ months**, stay **honest**, and **show the access no one else has**. Start this week, start imperfect, and improve every month. 🚴‍♂️
