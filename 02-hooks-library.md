# 2. Hooks Library (120+ Hooks)

Use these word for word or adapt them. A hook has three layers. Use all three at once:
1. **Visual hook** – what we SEE in frame 1 (movement, a huge plate of food, a sprint, a crash, a power meter)
2. **Text hook** – the on-screen text (at most 8 to 10 words, readable in 1 second)
3. **Spoken hook** – the first sentence you say

> Fill in the `[brackets]` with your real numbers. Real numbers do better than round numbers. "412 watts" is more believable and more interesting than "400 watts".
